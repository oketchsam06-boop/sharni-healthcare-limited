Who we are

Professional healthcare supply, centred on your needs.

Sharni Healthcare Limited is a healthcare products and pharmaceutical supply company serving healthcare providers and organizations in Kenya. We focus on reliable products and professional customer support for hospitals, clinics, laboratories, pharmacies, institutions, and other healthcare organizations.

Our product portfolio covers pharmaceuticals, medical consumables, laboratory supplies, surgical supplies, and PPE and infection prevention products.
